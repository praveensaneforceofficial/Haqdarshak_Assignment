Scheme Title: Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana
Department: Department of School Education, Madhya Pradesh

Brief Summary:
A state initiative to encourage girl child education and curb school dropout rates among teenage girls.

Benefits:
- Financial subsidy of Rs. 1,250 per month disbursed bi-annually (Rs. 7,500 every 6 months).

Eligibility:
- Girl students resident of Madhya Pradesh.
- Studying in Class 11 or 12 in state government schools.
- Family income below Rs. 2,50,000 per annum.

Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card
- School Identity Card / Bonafide Certificate
- Income Certificate
- Bank Account Details

Application Mode:
Online application via MyScheme integration or MP Online Kiosks.

Scheme Deadline / Window:
Application portal closes on 15th November 2026.
