GOVERNMENT OF MADHYA PRADESH
DEPARTMENT OF WOMEN AND CHILD DEVELOPMENT
NOTIFICATION No. WCD/2026/SCH-402
Bhopal, Dated: 15th January 2026

SUBJECT: Operational Guidelines for "Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana 2026"

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW:
The Department of Women and Child Development hereby announces the continuation and expansion of the "Mukhyamantri Balika Protsahan Yojana" for the financial year 2026-2027.

2. FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
Eligible girl students enrolled in higher secondary education shall receive direct financial assistance of Rs. 1,500 per month. The benefit amount will be disbursed on a quarterly basis (Rs. 4,500 per quarter) directly into the Aadhaar-linked bank account of the beneficiary.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- The applicant must be a domicile of Madhya Pradesh.
- The applicant must be a female student enrolled in Class 11 or Class 12 in a recognised government school.
- Annual family income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 2,50,000 per annum.
- Age of the applicant must be between 15 and 19 years as of 1st January 2026.
- The applicant must not be receiving scholarship benefits from any other state-funded scheme simultaneously.

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
i) Domicile Certificate of Madhya Pradesh.
ii) Aadhaar Card of the applicant (linked with active mobile number).
iii) Valid Income Certificate issued by Tahsildar / competent authority.
iv) Marksheet of Class 10 examination.
v) Bonafide Student Certificate issued by the Head of the Institution.
vi) Bank Passbook copy showing active DBT-enabled account.

5. APPLICATION PROCEDURE:
Applicants must submit their applications online through the official portal at https://wcd.mp.gov.in/balika-protsahan. Offline applications submitted to the District Programme Officer (DPO) will also be accepted at block-level Seva Kendras.

6. IMPORTANT DATES & DEADLINE:
Portal opening date: 1st February 2026.
Last date for submission of online application: 31st December 2026.
