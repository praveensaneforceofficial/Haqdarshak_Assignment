SUB: IMPLEMENTAT1ON OF "RAJASTHAN VRIDDHA PENSION YOJANA" (AMENDMENT 2026)

1. NAEM OF SCHEME:
Rajasthan Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Samman Pension Yojana.

2. DEPARTMENT:
Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of Rajasthan.

3. BENEF1T AMOUNT:
Monthly pension of Rs. 1,000 per month for beneficiaries aged 55 to 74 years, and Rs. 1,250 per month for beneficiaries aged 75 years and above. Disbursed monthly via Direct Benefit Transfer.

4. ELIGIB1LITY:
- Resident of Rajasthan.
- Women aged 55 years or older; Men aged 58 years or older.
- Total combined family annual income must not exceed Rs. 48,000/- per annum.
- Persons with no family support or having destitute status.

5. REQUIRED DOCS:
- Jan Aadhaar Card / Aadhaar Card
- Age proof / Voter ID
- Income self-declaration certificate attested by Patwari/Sarpanch
- Bank passbook

6. HOW TO APPLY:
Apply online through Emitra kiosks or RajSSP portal at https://rajssp.raj.nic.in.

7. DEADLINE:
Rolling scheme. No last date for application (Open throughout the year).
